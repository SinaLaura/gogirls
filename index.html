      New to Go?

      Perfect! We'll explain the rules and play your first games with you.

      Free to join

      Bring your own food and drinks, or get drinks on site for €1.50. If we bring snacks, a small donation (€1⁠–⁠2) is appreciated.

      At your own pace

      Come late, leave early or take a break. There are quiet rooms for a calm game or a moment to yourself.

      Just among us

      Cis, trans and inter women and non-binary people are welcome. We aim to be a safer space.

      What is Go?

      Go is a strategy board game for two players. The rules fit on an iron-on patch, but the game is so deep that you can keep discovering new things for a lifetime.
