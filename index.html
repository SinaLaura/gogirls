      What is Go Girls* Game Night?

      The Go Girls* Game Night is a monthly evening for women* who want to play Go (also known as Baduk or Weiqi), the ancient board game of black and white stones. Whether you've never placed a stone or play regularly: come by, play, chat and enjoy the evening with us.

      New to Go?

      Perfect! We'll explain the rules and play your first games with you.

      Language

      German and English – whatever suits you.

      Food & drinks

      Drinks are €1.50 on site. Feel free to bring snacks.

      The asterisk in Girls* includes everyone who identifies as a woman, including trans, inter and non-binary people.

      What is Go?

      Go is a strategy board game for two players. The rules fit on a napkin, but the game is so deep that you can keep discovering new things for a lifetime.
