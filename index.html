    What is Go Girls*?

    Go Girls* is a monthly game night for women* who want to play Go (also known as Baduk or Weiqi), the ancient board game of black and white stones. Whether you've never placed a stone or play regularly: come by, play, chat and enjoy the evening with us.

    New to Go?

    Perfect! We'll explain the rules and play your first games with you.

    Who can come?

    Women* of every age and level.

    Language

    German and English – whatever suits you.

    Food & drinks

    Drinks are €1.50 on site. Feel free to bring snacks.

    * The asterisk in Girls* includes everyone who identifies as a woman, including trans, inter and non-binary people.

    What is Go?

    Go is a strategy board game for two players. The rules fit on a napkin, but the game is so deep that you can keep discovering new things for a lifetime.

    • 2,500+ years old – it comes from China
    • 9×9 small board for beginners (the big one is 19×19)
    • 3 names Weiqi in China, Baduk in Korea, Igo in Japan
